# REQ-01 & REQ-07 & REQ-08 Validation Report

**Subject:** Independent drawer access, guidance, and rearward retention.  
**Method:** Manual operation of the assembled three-drawer prototype.

## Checks and Decisions

Dimensions are nominal CAD values. The prototype uses two guided tiers and one earlier unguided tier.

| Check | Parameters | Observation | My decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent access | Three 180 × 140 × 48 mm drawers; nominal frame opening: 182 mm wide | Each drawer could be removed while the others remained installed | Keep independently removable drawers for routine care. |
| Side guidance | R3.5 center guide: 10 mm front-to-back width | The unguided tier allowed sideways movement and jammed more often. Guides improved insertion, but occasional manual realignment remained | Add a simple center guide to each side frame. |
| Rear retention | One 174 × 12 × 6 mm bar, installed vertically at the rear center and bonded with cyanoacrylate | The bar stopped rearward travel and felt firm under an initial hand push | Use one bar across the three tiers. Accept partial rear-vent obstruction as a current trade-off requiring observation. |
| Finger pull | v1.4: 70 mm span and 15 mm nominal finger clearance | The finger pull printed with supports starting from the build plate | Keep the recessed finger grip and use supports where needed. |

**Slicer estimates:** One supported v1.4 drawer: 116.78 g / 5 h 40 min. Rear bar: 8.24 g / 35 min.

## Conclusion

Independent access and initial rear retention worked in manual use. Drawer guidance improved, but the no-realignment target is not fully demonstrated. Jam frequency, repeated operating cycles, and adhesive durability were not measured.
