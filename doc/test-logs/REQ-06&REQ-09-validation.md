# REQ-06 & REQ-09 Validation Report

**Subject:** Removable top cover and carrying handle.  
**Method:** Manual installation, removal, and ordinary cover handling.  
**Equipment:** Bambu P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle; PETG HF.

## Checks and Decisions

Parameters below describe the v1.1 CAD model; clearances and strength were not instrumentally measured.

| Feature | Parameters | Observation | My decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover location | 202 × 154 mm cover; two diagonal sockets with 0.15 mm per-side clearance, two with 0.30 mm relief | The cover could be installed and removed by hand | Use light locating friction with extra clearance at two sockets to reduce binding. |
| Cover retention | Two removable side-lock rails engaging four tongue passages | The rails could be fitted and removed by hand | Use positive engagement for retention while keeping the cover removable for expansion. |
| Handle | 114 × 12 × 7 mm grip bar; two 8 mm-wide end supports; 1.20 mm cover skin with reinforcing ribs | No obvious flex during ordinary cover handling; the loaded rack was not lifted | Replace the bulkier concept with two end supports and local reinforcement. |

**Cover, handle, and two side locks:** 90.12 g and 5 h 15 min — slicer estimates.

## Conclusion

I kept the lighter v1.1 cover after the initial fit and handling checks. These checks support printability and manual assembly. They do not establish a carrying load for the complete rack. Loaded lifting and repeated lock/removal cycles remain untested.
