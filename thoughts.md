# My Experience Working with AI

In this project, I wanted to build a mealworm habitat and see how far I could go with AI-assisted design. During development, I realized that some changes would be faster to make myself. I still continued with AI because I wanted to understand where it worked well and where it needed more guidance.

## What Worked Well

AI was useful for generating small test models with different parameters. For example, before printing the full frame, I could print several connection samples and compare their fit. This helped me find problems before spending more time and material on larger parts.

I also think AI can help people who have less experience with testing and version management. It suggested testing individual features, keeping previous versions, and recording the results. It could quickly provide several options for comparison, but I still needed to print and test them to decide which one worked.

## What Took More Time Than Expected

The C connection was one example. After testing its fit, I expected AI to keep the confirmed dimensions and use that connection as the basis for the frame and tier connections. I understood that a different application might need another check, but the result we already had should not be lost.

However, AI sometimes introduced a different arrangement or added separate locking pieces. My intention was to put the pegs directly on the crossbars and the sockets in the side frames. At the same time, I was considering how to assemble the parts and place them on the print bed. AI did not consistently include all of these points without further instructions.

I had to explain the same requirements several times. Clearer prompts helped, but part of the conversation was only correcting its understanding. This used extra time and tokens, and it was especially frustrating when usage was limited.

Once I knew exactly what needed to change, directly editing the CAD model was faster than explaining it again and checking whether AI had changed anything else. I noticed this during the project, particularly for small changes to details that had already been tested.

## What I Would Change Next Time

I would still use AI for initial ideas and test models with different parameters. For integration and small CAD changes, I would do more of the work directly once the design was clear.

I would also try giving AI a short record of confirmed dimensions, tested connections, and what could be changed. I want to see whether this reduces repeated explanations.

This is my experience from one project. I would need to try other models, platforms, and tools before making a broader judgment. I also have not tested whether a higher subscription level would solve these problems.
